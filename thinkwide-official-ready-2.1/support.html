Support terms in detail

Key terms that govern the support services for our products and solutions.

1. Scope of support What is included in our support services.

ThinkLab is a cloud-based laboratory management platform designed to manage patient registration, billing, sample collection, testing, result entry, report generation and other day-to-day lab operations through one connected system.

2. Support hours Our standard availability and working hours.

Yes. ThinkLab supports multi-location operations, helping laboratories manage branches, collection centres, users and workflows through a centralised platform.

3. Service levels (SLA) Response and resolution timelines by priority.

Yes. ThinkLab supports interfacing with compatible laboratory analyzers, allowing test results to flow directly into the laboratory workflow and reducing repetitive manual data entry.

4. Issue reporting How to raise a support request and what to share.

ThinkLab works with the Collector App to manage assigned collections, patient details, visit status, sample collection, barcodes, payments and sample handover.

5. Exclusions Items not covered under standard support.

Yes. Through connected patient services, patients can book tests, manage family profiles, access available reports and maintain their diagnostic records digitally.

6. Updates and maintenance Product updates, patches and planned maintenance.

7. Customer responsibilities Your role in enabling timely support and resolution.

Priority levels

Response and resolution targets

Priority Meaning First response Target resolution
P1 · Critical Complete outage, core operations inaccessible, or major billing failure with no workaround Within 1 hour 4–6 hours
P2 · Significant A major function unavailable, operations continue in part Within 4 business hours 1 business day
P3 · Less significant Secondary feature issue or one with a reasonable workaround Within 1 business day 2–3 business days
P4 · Minimal Cosmetic or informational issue Within 2 business days 5 business days

"First response" means a person from support contacting you with a triage plan, not an automated receipt. "Resolution" means core function restored or a workaround in place. Targets depend on timely reporting and cooperation and are governed by your signed SLA.

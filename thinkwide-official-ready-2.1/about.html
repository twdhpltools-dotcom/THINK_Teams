About ThinkWide

We build technology for better diagnostic healthcare

Thinkwide Digital Health Pvt. Ltd. is a Kolkata-based healthcare technology company, incorporated in 2022. We make software for diagnostic labs and imaging centres, and run digital marketing for healthcare brands.

Our mission

Make every diagnostic centre run like the best one in its city.

Accurate reports, delivered fast, explained clearly, from a business that knows its numbers.

What we offer

ThinkLab for diagnostics, ThinkRay for radiology and ThinkDigital for healthcare marketing, with connected apps and modules.

People

Product, implementation, support and marketing teams working from Sector V, Kolkata.

Where we are

Head office at SDF Building, Sector V, Bidhannagar, Kolkata.

What we value

How we work with labs

Clinical respect

Software supports clinicians; it never replaces their judgement.

Plain answers

Clear pricing, clear support levels, clear data terms.

On the ground

Long-term partners

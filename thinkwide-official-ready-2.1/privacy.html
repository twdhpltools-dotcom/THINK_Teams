Understanding how we handle personal data

This notice explains ThinkWide's roles, the types of information involved in our services, and the safeguards and rights relevant to personal data. Please read the sections below for the full policy.

1.1Identity and capacity

This Privacy Policy is issued by ThinkWide Digital Health Private Limited (“Thinkwide”). Where Thinkwide determines the purpose and means of processing personal data collected through its corporate website, demonstration forms, customer contracting, account administration, recruitment or support, it acts as a Data Fiduciary. Where a healthcare customer determines the purpose and means of processing patient records in ThinkLab or ThinkRay and instructs Thinkwide to process such records, Thinkwide generally acts as that customer’s Data Processor. A particular processing activity may require a separate role assessment.

1.2Scope and applicable instruments

This Policy applies to Thinkwide-controlled processing of identifiable individuals’ digital personal data and describes, for transparency, relevant processor activities. Customer patient-facing notices and lawful-basis determinations remain the responsibility of the applicable customer unless otherwise agreed or required by law. This Policy operates alongside applicable customer contracts and the laws in force at the relevant time; it does not replace a patient-specific consent notice.

1.3Categories and sources

Depending on the relevant transaction, data may include individual/business contact and identity particulars; enquiry and demonstration submissions; employee/applicant details; authorised-user identities and permissions; account, commercial, invoice and payment references; support correspondence; usage and security event records; IP/device/browser information; and, solely within customer-hosted workflows, patient demographic, clinical, diagnostic imaging, billing and report data. Collection occurs from individuals, customer representatives, authorised users, systems and approved service providers to the extent necessary for the relevant purpose.

1.4Purpose and lawful ground

Thinkwide uses its own business data to respond to requests, provide demonstrations, administer contractual accounts, supply and secure services, handle billing and taxes, provide support, monitor lawful access, manage recruitment, comply with legal obligations and send service communications. Marketing contact will be undertaken only where lawfully permitted, with appropriate consent or opt-out arrangements. Any consent request shall be specific, informed, affirmatively given and distinguishable from service terms as required under applicable law. Merely viewing this Policy is not treated as consent to unrelated processing.

1.5Customer-controlled data

Thinkwide shall process customer patient data for the documented purposes of hosting, storing, transmission, retrieval, permitted support, security and other agreed service functions, subject to the customer’s instructions, applicable law and an appropriate processing agreement. Thinkwide shall not independently determine patient marketing use or disclose patient records for unrelated purposes. This allocation of responsibility does not excuse a party from obligations directly imposed upon it by law.

1.6Disclosure and sub-processors

Thinkwide may disclose information to personnel with a need to know, customer-approved/contractually authorised hosting and technology providers, payment and communications providers, professional advisers and authorities where lawfully required. Access shall be limited to the relevant purpose and governed by appropriate confidentiality and contractual conditions. A current list of material service-provider categories and relevant cross-border processing arrangements shall be maintained internally and disclosed where legally required.

1.7Security, retention and rights

Thinkwide shall maintain reasonable technical and organisational safeguards proportionate to risk, as further addressed in its Information Security Statement and binding contracts. Retention shall follow the approved record-class schedule, lawful erasure obligations and mandatory preservation periods. Eligible individuals may contact [VERIFIED PRIVACY EMAIL] concerning applicable access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal, grievance and nomination rights, subject to commencement, verification, applicable exemptions and law.

1.8Children, transfers and changes

The relevant customer remains responsible for appropriately establishing the basis for processing minors’ patient records and any required parental/guardian verification, including statutory exemptions. Cross-border transfers, if any, shall remain subject to applicable restrictions. Material changes to this Policy will be published with the effective date and additional notices/consents supplied where required. Have a privacy-related enquiry?

For requests involving patient records held by a diagnostic centre or healthcare provider, please first contact that organisation. For ThinkWide-controlled data enquiries, reach our team through our contact page until the designated privacy contact has been confirmed.

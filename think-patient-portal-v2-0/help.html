Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a test or appointment?

Choose the required test or doctor, select your preferred date and time, add the booking to your cart and complete the booking.

How can I view or download my reports?

Open your Lab Booking Summary, select the required booking and use the report download option once your report is ready.

How does Home Collection work?

Select Home Collection while booking, choose your preferred address and slot, and our assigned collector will visit you for sample collection.

When will I receive my refund?

Once a refund is approved, the amount may be credited to your wallet or original payment method according to the applicable refund process.
